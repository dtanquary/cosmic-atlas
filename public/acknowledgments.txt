NGC 4026 AND NGC 3982 RESOLVED PROFILES

Imaging shape measurements in the DESI source are credited to the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys and the Siena Galaxy Atlas (SGA-2020). SGA reference: Moustakas, Lang, et al. (2023), "Siena Galaxy Atlas 2020", https://www.legacysurvey.org/sga/sga2020/ .

The Siena Galaxy Atlas was made possible by funding support from the U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science, Office of High Energy Physics under Award Number DE-SC0020086 and from the National Science Foundation under grant AST-1616414.

DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys: DECaLS, BASS and MzLS; imaging obtained at the Blanco, Bok and Mayall telescopes, with pipeline processing supported by NOIRLab and LBNL. The full scientific publication acknowledgment, funding credits and SGA acknowledgment are available at https://www.legacysurvey.org/acknowledgment/ . Publications using these data should include the acknowledgments specified there.

Additional processing: two original DESI imaging profiles are approximated by positive Gaussian components, placed at their original redshift-derived catalog positions, and deprojected under an assumed oblate shape. Sky angle and apparent size follow the measured Tractor shapes. Depth, reflected tilt choice, exposure and colors are model/display assumptions. NGC 4026 remains smooth. NGC 3982 adds illustrative spiral arms and light knots; their pitch, phase, handedness and brightness are not measurements, and knots are not individual observed stars. Hubble morphology reference: https://esahubble.org/images/opo1036a/ . No telescope image is redistributed; comparison links open the original Legacy Surveys viewer.

Filtered SGA downloads: https://sga.legacysurvey.org/ . Input checksums and exact matched identities are retained in data/galaxy-search.json.

OpenNGC database: Copyright Mattia Verga, https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC . Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . A copy is included at licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt.

CATALOG-WIDE GALAXY MODELS

Imaging profiles are extracted for all 14,140,375 accepted DR1 observations, retaining the original catalog ordering, positions, distances, and identifiers. Streamed profile sidecars recover angular radius, ellipticity and imaging-fit type. 12,097,577 rows have usable measured shapes; 2,042,798 use explicitly labeled illustrative shapes with an assumed 5 kpc comoving half-light radius and unknown orientation.

3,128 exact named central matches have recognized visual morphological types from the SGA-2020 MORPHTYPE field (credited to HyperLEDA), with OpenNGC filling missing recognized types. These visual classifications are distinct from DESI's Tractor imaging model labels. SGA field documentation: https://www.legacysurvey.org/sga/sga2020/ . HyperLEDA: https://leda.univ-lyon1.fr/ . Broad morphology descriptions: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/galaxies/types/ .

Without a recorded visual classification, display families are labeled approximations, not measured Hubble types. Galaxy depth, reflected tilt choice, all bars/arms/clumps, colors and exposure are illustrative. Generic smooth profiles use a quantized Sersic Gaussian library. Original telescope images are not redistributed. See docs/galaxy-detail.md for fitting, deprojection and fallback assumptions.

The derived model manifest and profile sidecars include adapted OpenNGC classifications and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the same OpenNGC credit and license above. Original DESI data retain their CC BY 4.0 license. Input checksums and processing descriptions are retained in the model manifest and preparation scripts.

INDEPENDENT NEARBY GALAXIES

The six-entry nearby layer uses adopted numerical measurements from McConnachie, A. W. (2012), AJ, 144, 4, and its January 2021 compilation: https://www.cadc-ccda.hia-iha.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/en/community/nearby/ . Coordinates and selected half-light radii/ellipses are credited to that compilation and its referenced studies. M32's RR Lyrae distance is attributed there to Fiorentino et al. (2010).

M31, M33 and NGC 205 tip-of-the-red-giant-branch distances: McConnachie et al. (2005), https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0410489 .
Large Magellanic Cloud eclipsing-binary distance: Pietrzynski et al. (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.08096 .
Small Magellanic Cloud eclipsing-binary distance: Graczyk et al. (2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08754 .
M31 disk scale and projected flattening: Courteau et al. (2011), https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3564 .
M33 K-band disk scale: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21778.x ; rescaled from that work's adopted distance.

Additional projected axis ratios, sky angles and aliases derive from Mattia Verga's OpenNGC, under CC BY-SA 4.0, as credited above. Adapted OpenNGC shape fields and the combined nearby reference are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, preserving the research provenance of other numerical measurements. Processing converts sexagesimal coordinates, distance moduli, angular sizes and ellipses; fits illustrative smooth Gaussian profiles; and adds explicit missing-shape assumptions. Source snapshot and input-excerpt SHA-256 values are retained in src/data/nearby-sources.json and the generated nearby-galaxies.json. See docs/nearby-galaxies.md for exact choices.

The layer does not alter or globally deduplicate DESI observations. Depth, ambiguous near side, arms/clumps, colors and exposure are illustrative; the Clouds have explicitly assumed model sizes, and LMC orientation is unknown. The six nearby entries are counted separately. These adopted values are not a claim of the latest scientific consensus.
